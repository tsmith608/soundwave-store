# Competitor Research — September 2026

**Method and limits.** Live web research on 28 Sep 2026. This environment's network policy blocked direct page fetches for Etsy, Artsy Voiceprint, Positive Prints and Canvas Vows, so data comes from search-engine extracts of those pages and from third-party review sites. Prices and review counts appear only where a source showed them; "n/a" means not visible, not zero. Before finalising pricing, the owner should spend 30 minutes manually checking the five most direct competitors (§4).

**Legend.** **Fact** = stated by a cited source. **Observed** = pattern across several sources. **Hypothesis** / **Recommendation** as labelled.

## 3. What the landscape tells us

**Price ladder (observed).**

| Tier | Price |
|---|---|
| Digital | $9–10 |
| Unframed print | $15–40 (UK £15–28) |
| Framed | $50–110 |
| Canvas | $79–100 |
| Large statement | $150–300 |

Framed typographic keepsakes cluster around **$70–110** (Mapiful ~$72, Canvas Vows $79–100, Modern Memory ~$110, Schafer $80 "sale").

**Playback is table stakes (fact).** Almost every direct competitor offers playback, via a QR card (Artsy Voiceprint), a printed QR (Schafer, Positive Prints), a Spotify code (UK sellers) or a proprietary app (Soundwave Art). Plain camera-scan QR with no app and no Spotify dependency is the friendliest version, and it also works for voicemails, which Spotify can't host.

**Visual sameness (observed).** The dominant look is a centred waveform + script font + lyrics + QR, on canvas or wood. The main variants are watercolour splashes, photo collages for memorials, and rustic signs. **None of the direct competitors we found sells a composition where the design stands on its own as art.** The adjacent winners (The Night Sky, Blim & Blum) prove that restrained, typographic, well-made pieces earn thousands of reviews.

**Use-case evidence (observed, strength ranked).** First dance / wedding song (dedicated collections at Artsy Voiceprint, Positive Prints, Canvas Vows, Phoenix Art, NOTHS sellers; Blim & Blum's volume) → memorial voicemail (Etsy has several dedicated market pages; Schafer, TrueLove and Artsy Voiceprint collections) → baby heartbeat (Artsy Voiceprint, Abide and Grace, Etsy) → pet memorial (Etsy, Artsy Voiceprint, a new August 2026 entrant) → general "voice recording gift" (grandparent/long-distance: recordable frames).

**Recurring weaknesses we can exploit.**
1. The design burden is put on the customer (editors, font pickers).
2. Fake-discount pricing ("reg. $175.99").
3. Craft and farmhouse aesthetics that don't suit modern interiors.
4. Playback locked to an app or Spotify.
5. Weak product imagery.
6. Manual proofing workflows (Dropbox links, emailed proofs, 3–7 days).

## 4. Implications and recommendations

1. **Compete on the finished design, not on price.** Hypothesis: a customer comparing us with a $20 Etsy print has to see the difference in one thumbnail. That's why each design is a complete composition (see art-direction-2026.md).
2. **Price in the adjacent-leader band:**

   | Size | Print | Framed |
   |---|---|---|
   | 12×16 (hero) | $49 | $99 |
   | 8×10 | $35 | $69 |
   | 18×24 | $65 | $149 |

   Framed prices sit just above Mapiful and Canvas Vows, justified by design and playback. Validate with the owner's supplier costs (unit-economics.md).
3. **Make 12×16 the default gift size** (The Night Sky's most popular gift size) and state clearly whether an item is framed. Its customers complain about exactly this ambiguity.
4. **Make the instant preview the proof.** Competitors email proofs over days; our preview is the print file.
5. **Show real reviews only, and collect them from day one.** Review volume is the adjacent leaders' moat.
6. **Manual checks for the owner before launch:** current prices and bestsellers at Artsy Voiceprint, Positive Prints and the top three Etsy listings for "custom soundwave art" and "voicemail memorial gift". This environment could only see them through search extracts.
