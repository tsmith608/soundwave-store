# Pricing review (2 Oct 2026)

**Verdict:** our prices are in the sweet spot. Framed 12×16 at **$99** sits in the middle of the premium band. That's above Etsy's discount sellers and below the most expensive custom brands, and it leaves an estimated ~$36 contribution per order. Keep it. There are three adjustments below; the main open risk is that our **print and frame costs are still estimates**.

**Three tiers:**
1. **Budget, $15–60.** Etsy: a generic waveform, a QR code, a cheap frame, permanent "sales".
2. **Premium personalised, $70–170.** Bespoken, Artsy Voiceprint, Mapiful, Grafomap.
3. **Us, $69–149 framed.** Inside tier 2, priced below Bespoken's custom framed work. We offer more than either tier: two designed collections, a real moon phase, plant growth driven by the recording, a code that plays the recording, and a preview of exactly what gets printed.

US shipping is included. That's correct for gifts, where a surprise shipping cost at checkout kills conversion.

## Recommendations

1. **Don't lower prices to compete with Etsy.** Those buyers want $20. Our buyer is choosing something to hang for decades, and a $25 price would signal "cheap". Compete on the design, the moon, the playback and the trust signals.
2. **Hold $99 for framed 12×16 unless Prodigi's real landed cost is above about $60.** If it is, go to **$109**, which is still below Bespoken's custom range. Get the real cost first (it's on the launch checklist).
3. **Add a "copy for family" option at checkout** (a second identical print, about 30% off). Memorial orders often need copies for siblings; it lifts order value with no extra design work. This needs a small checkout change; I can build it.
4. **No percentage sales.** For Black Friday, if anything, offer a **free upgrade** from print to framed for a set amount, or free gift wrap. Etsy-style permanent "50% off" trains people to wait.
5. **Order-by dates are a selling point.** Write them on product pages so people pay full price to make the deadline.
