## Offers (O): guidance

- **Default:** no percentage discounts. Competitors' constant "sales" are a weakness (competitor research).
- **BFCM (recommended):** "**Framed for the print price + $30**" on 12×16 (i.e. $79 instead of $99). Mid-case contribution ≈ $17, still positive (unit-economics §3). Or free upgrade to 18×24 framed at the 12×16 price (contribution ≈ $1, **not** recommended).
- **Family copy:** a second copy of the same design and recording at −30%. Especially for memorial orders; raises AOV at low cost.
- **Never** run fake countdown timers or strike-through "was" prices that were never charged.
