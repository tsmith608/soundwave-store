# Organic Content Strategy — Q4 2026

**Scope:** TikTok first; the same edits go natively to Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts; mockups go to Pinterest.
**Equipment:** a phone, a desk lamp, one framed sample of each design (see owner-review for sample cost), and screen recordings of the studio.

Earlier documents (`CONTENT_SCRIPTS.md`, `docs/UGC_SCRIPTS_50.md`) were written for the old product. This plan supersedes them for Q4.

---

## 1. What the platforms reward right now

| Signal | Evidence | Implication for us |
|---|---|---|
| Completion and rewatch rate are the top TikTok ranking signals; follower count matters less, so small accounts can reach new people | [Stack Influence](https://stackinfluence.com/blog/tiktok-algorithm-2026-what-creators-must-know), [Social Rescue](https://www.socialrescue.com/blogs/how-the-tiktok-algorithm-works-in-2026) | Put the payoff (the sound, the reveal) in the first 2 seconds and loop cleanly; most videos 12–30 s |
| TikTok down-ranks low-effort reposts and watermarked content | [Stack Influence](https://stackinfluence.com/blog/tiktok-algorithm-2026-what-creators-must-know) | Film originals; never repost other creators' reactions |
| TikTok is a search engine (~1.5B searches/day) | [Stack Influence](https://stackinfluence.com/blog/tiktok-algorithm-2026-what-creators-must-know) | Say the searchable phrase out loud and on-screen ("how to save a voicemail", "first dance song gift") |
| Instagram Reels ranks by watch time, then **sends per reach**, then likes; an originality fingerprint check reduces reach for near-duplicates of the last 90 days | [Dataslayer (Mosseri signals)](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers), [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/instagram-reels-algorithm) | Upload the raw edit to IG (not the TikTok export with watermark); write captions that invite sending ("send this to your sister") |
| Face-to-camera beats slideshows and text-only posts; day-in-the-life small-business videos massively outperform category averages | [Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/small-business-tiktok/) | The owner on camera, briefly, at least twice a week |
| "Packing orders" ASMR is an established small-business format | [LitCommerce](https://litcommerce.com/blog/tiktok-trends-for-businesses/), [TikTok discover](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/asmr-packing-orders) | Works once real orders exist; pre-launch, use sample prints |
| A grandmother's-voicemail soundwave tattoo went viral | [Wikipedia summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soundwave_tattoos) | Proof that "a loved one's voice, made visible" travels. Handle with care. |
| Pinterest's 2026 Wedding Report (7B+ wedding searches) lists personalised keepsakes and custom artwork among top gift ideas | [Pinterest Newsroom](https://newsroom.pinterest.com/news/wedding-trend-report-2026/) | Pin every mockup; Pinterest is slower but compounds into Q1 wedding season |

## 2. Formats: relevant or not

| Format | Relevant? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Audio → artwork reveal** (screen recording) | **Core** | Shows the mechanism in 5 seconds; the sound itself is the hook |
| **"I turned ___ into ___"** | **Core** | Clear transformation; searchable |
| **Scan-the-QR moment** (phone at the frame, voice plays) | **Core** | The "magic" moment competitors rarely film well |
| **Practical how-to** (save a voicemail, get audio from a wedding video) | **Core** | High saves and sends; ranks in TikTok search; helps even non-buyers |
| **Design explainer** ("every leaf is a sound") | High | Curiosity plus differentiation; only we can make it |
| **Packing / framing an order** | High once orders exist | Trust and ASMR; show the real frame and paper |
| **Comment-reply series** ("comment your date, I'll show you the moon") | High | Built for engagement; each reply is a new video |
| **Founder story / day-in-the-life** | Medium | Builds trust; keep it honest and short |
| **Gift recipient reactions** | Medium, **only real ones** | Strong when real. Ask customers after delivery, with consent. **Never stage or pay for a fake reaction without clear disclosure** (FTC endorsement rules). |
| **Creator UGC** | Later | Worth it once organic proves a message; see paid doc |
| Trend-audio lip-syncs, generic "POV" skits | Low | Off-brand, and they bury our own sound |

## 3. Voice and ethics

- **Show the product, say what it is.** No "this will make you cry" bait.
- **Memorial content:** only with the family's explicit permission, or using the owner's own family recordings, or clearly labelled examples. Never use a stranger's grief as a hook.
- Every sample in these videos is either the owner's own recording or clearly an example. If it's an actor or a friend, say so in the caption ("example with my sister's voice").

## 4. Thirty concepts

Each has an ID for UTM tracking (`utm_content=c01`, etc.).

**Shot key:**
- **SR** = screen recording of the studio on phone
- **CU** = close-up of the framed print
- **FTC** = face to camera

**Target lengths:** 10–20 s for reveals, 25–45 s for how-tos.

### A. Reveals (the product mechanism)

**c01 — "This is what my mom's voicemail looks like"** · Memorial · 14 s
- **Hook (0–2 s):** play 3 seconds of a real voicemail over black; text "this is what my mom's voicemail looks like."
- **Shots:** SR upload → In Memoriam preview draws → CU of the framed print on a shelf.
- **CTA:** "Link in bio if you have one you want to keep."
- **Emotion:** tenderness.

**c02 — "Our first dance, but make it a record sleeve"** · Wedding · 12 s
- **Hook:** first line of the song + text "our first dance song → wall art."
- **Shots:** SR typing song and names into Liner Notes; the waveform snaps to the real song shape; CU of the black frame.
- **CTA:** "Makes a good first anniversary gift (it's paper)."

**c03 — "Every leaf is a moment of the song"** · Anniversary / botanical · 18 s
- **Hook:** macro CU tracing one leaf; VO "this leaf is the word 'forever'."
- **Shots:** SR side by side, a recording scrubbing → leaves growing; pull back to the framed Herbarium.
- **CTA:** "Pick your song, we grow the plant."

**c04 — "The moon on the night we met"** · Anniversary · 15 s
- **Hook:** text "what did the moon look like the night you met?"
- **Shots:** SR date picker changes; the moon phase changes live across 3 dates; land on theirs.
- **CTA:** "Comment your date." → feeds c20.

**c05 — "A 4-second voicemail is enough"** · Memorial · 12 s
- **Hook:** play "Hi, it's Dad, call me back."
- **Shots:** SR upload → fine line appears → text "four seconds."
- **CTA:** "You don't need a long one."

**c06 — "Same song, five designs"** · Wedding · 15 s
- **Hook:** one song playing.
- **Shots:** SR tap through all 5 designs with the same recording; quick cuts on the beat.
- **CTA:** "Which one would you hang?" (comment prompt).

**c07 — "Baby's heartbeat → nursery print"** · Baby · 14 s
- **Hook:** doppler heartbeat audio.
- **Shots:** SR upload → Herbarium Blush → CU in a white frame.
- **CTA:** "For the grandparents, too."

**c08 — "Dog's bark on the wall"** · Pet · 12 s
- **Hook:** a single bark (owner's pet).
- **Shots:** SR upload → In Memoriam Linen with the pet's name → CU.
- **CTA:** "Every video of your dog has this in it."

### B. The QR moment

**c09 — "Scan it"** · All · 10 s
- **Hook:** a phone camera pointed at the frame; the recording starts playing out loud (a first-dance song).
- **Shots:** one continuous take.
- **Text:** "no app."
- **CTA:** "It plays from any phone."

**c10 — "Grandma scanning it"** · Family · 15 s (real family member)
- **Hook:** an older relative holds a phone up, hears a grandchild's voice.
- **Shots:** handheld, natural.
- **CTA:** "Record the kids for Christmas."

**c11 — "Guess what this plays"** · Engagement · 10 s
- **Hook:** CU of an unnamed print.
- **Shots:** pause; scan; reveal sound (e.g. a proposal).
- **CTA:** "Guess before you scan."

### C. Practical how-tos (search + saves)

- **Hook:** "Carriers delete voicemails, sometimes after a few weeks. Here's how to keep it."
- **Shots:** iPhone steps SR (Phone → Voicemail → Share → Save to Files).
- **CTA:** "Full guide at the link," pointing to `/how-to-save-a-voicemail`.

**c13 — Same, for Android** · 35 s
- **Hook:** "Android version, because everyone asked."
- **Shots:** Phone by Google → Voicemail → ⋮ → Share; plus the screen-record fallback.

**c14 — "How to get the audio out of your wedding video"** · Wedding / utility · 30 s
- **Hook:** "Your first dance is in a video somewhere."
- **Shots:** SR finding the video → share → upload straight into the studio (videos are accepted).
- **CTA:** link.

**c15 — "What to record for Grandma this Thanksgiving"** · Family · 25 s · FTC

- **Shots:** 3 prompt ideas (her laugh, her saying your name, her recipe instructions).

### D. Design point of view (differentiation)

## 5. Cadence and production

- **Batch film** twice a week, 60–90 minutes each: 5–7 videos.
- **Post 1 per day** on TikTok, rising to 2 per day from Black Friday.
  - Research suggests 3–5 per day for small accounts ([Stack Influence](https://stackinfluence.com/blog/tiktok-algorithm-2026-what-creators-must-know)), but a solo founder can't sustain that at this quality. **Consistency beats volume.**
- **IG Reels:** same videos, native upload, 4–5 per week.
- **Shorts:** same videos, 3 per week.
- **Pinterest:** 5 pins per week from `public/mockups/` and new renders.
- **Weekly review (Sunday, 20 minutes):**
  - Per video: views, completion %, shares/sends, profile visits, link clicks (UTM).
  - Keep a sheet with one row per concept id.
  - A concept earns **3 more variants** if it beats the account median on completion **and** shares.
  - Anything that clears **2× median completion and ≥ 1% share rate** is a Spark-Ads candidate (see paid-acquisition-q4-2026.md).
