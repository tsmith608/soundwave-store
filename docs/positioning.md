## 3. Decision

**Primary positioning (recommendation):**

> **"Your song, your vows, a voice you love — made into art for the wall."**
> *Upload the recording. Choose a finished design. Scan the print and hear it again.*

- It names the three sources people actually have (a song, spoken words, a voice), not the technology.
- It promises **art**: the design is the differentiator (competitor-research §3).
- "Hear it again" is the functional benefit, stated plainly. No invented sentiment.

**Why not lead with "custom soundwave art."** It's the category word, and useful in SEO titles and product copy, but as a headline it commoditises us against $15–20 Etsy prints and DIY generators ([WaveVisual from $9](https://wavevisual.com/pricing)). We keep it in page titles and meta descriptions where search intent uses it.

**Why not lead with grief ("preserve their voice").** It's the most emotionally powerful use case, but a homepage led by loss would alienate the larger wedding and anniversary gift audience. Memorial gets its own page, its own design and its own content track, where the urgent, practical framing ("save the voicemail before it's deleted") is honest and helpful.

## 5. SEO strategy

**Principles:**
- Few pages, each genuinely different and useful.
- No keyword-swapped variants (Google's [scaled content abuse](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) policy; see also [analysis](https://gautamkhorana.com/blog/scaled-content-abuse-programmatic-seo/)).

**Built and indexable:**

| URL | Target intents (to validate with volumes) |
|---|---|
| `/` | soundwave art, custom sound wave art, song wall art |
| `/wedding-song-art` | wedding song wall art, first dance song print, first dance gift, wedding song sound wave |
| `/voicemail-memorial-art` | voicemail memorial gift, voicemail art, memorial voice recording gift, loved one's voice gift |
| `/anniversary-sound-wave-gift` | anniversary sound wave gift, paper anniversary gift song, song anniversary gift |
| `/pet-memorial-sound-art` | pet memorial sound wave, dog bark sound wave art |
| `/designs` | brand / navigational |
| `/gifts/first-baby-heartbeat-soundwave-art`, `/gifts/baby-first-laugh-soundwave-art`, `/gifts/proposal-audio-soundwave-art` | kept from the earlier pSEO set, still indexable |

**Consolidated (noindex or canonical):**
- 17 anniversary-year pages (e.g. "6th iron anniversary soundwave art"): **noindex**. The product isn't iron. They remain reachable for anyone who has a link.
- `first-dance-song-…`, `wedding-vow-…`, `pet-memorial-…`, `celebration-of-life-…`, `1st-paper-anniversary-…`: **canonical** to the new intent pages.

**Evaluated but not built:**
- `/custom-song-wall-art`: duplicates `/wedding-song-art` and `/`.
- `/voice-recording-gift`: splits between memorial and living-voice intents. Covered by `/voicemail-memorial-art` plus the homepage. Build later only if volume data shows a distinct audience.

**Next content to build** (informational intent that competitors already capture: [LifeEcho](https://lifeecho.org/blog/how-to-preserve-a-loved-ones-voicemail), [Memory Murals](https://memorymurals.com/journal/save-deceased-loved-ones-voicemail), [Funeral.com](https://funeral.com/blogs/the-journal/how-to-save-voicemails-as-mp3-iphone-and-android-methods-plus-backup-tips)):

- **Guide:** "How to get the audio from a wedding video" (for couples without a clean song file).

**Technical:** sitemap and robots added; FAQ structured data on intent pages; canonical URLs; `/l/*`, `/order/*` and `/dev/*` are disallowed and noindex.

**Off-page:**
- Pinterest is the natural discovery channel for wedding and home prints. Pin every mockup with keyword-descriptive titles.
- Submit to 2026 gift guides (wedding blogs, grief-support resources) with a sample print. Samples cost money and are listed in owner-review.
